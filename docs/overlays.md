# Domain overlays

The universal 22 stay stable. An overlay adds domain evidence; it does not
renumber the factors or silently weaken their boundaries. Record active overlay
identifiers in the assessment’s `overlays` array and attach their evidence to the
relevant factor entries.

## AI and model-mediated systems

- Factor XI: model/provider contract, output schema, refusal and uncertainty semantics.
- Factor XII: prompt/tool authorization, injection and exfiltration tests, human override.
- Factor XIII: task-quality and safety objectives, drift and evaluation lineage.
- Factor XIV: model, dataset, prompt, and evaluator provenance.
- Factor XVI: training/inference purpose limits, retention, memorization, and deletion.
- Factor XVIII: shadow, cohort, rollback, and kill-switch evidence.

## Local-first systems

- Factor IV: sync service is replaceable and offline behavior is explicit.
- Factor VI: local durable state and cloud coordination have named ownership.
- Factor XI: merge semantics and conflict visibility are part of the contract.
- Factor XVI: local copies, exports, backups, revocation, and deletion are governed.
- Factor XIX: old and new clients coexist across long offline windows.

## Mobile and edge clients

- Factor III: platform entitlements and environment-specific values are validated.
- Factor VIII: backgrounding, eviction, low power, and interrupted updates are safe.
- Factor XII: local secret storage, permissions, and hostile-device boundaries are tested.
- Factor XVIII: store rollout and server exposure can stop independently.
- Factor XIX: server contracts tolerate the supported client-version window.

## Regulated and safety-critical systems

- Factor V: release approval and artifact custody are immutable and attributable.
- Factor X: exceptional procedures are authorized, rehearsed, and auditable.
- Factor XII: hazards, threats, and fail-safe/fail-secure behavior are explicit.
- Factor XVI: legal purpose, retention, residency, subject agency, and deletion are evidenced.
- Factor XX: accountable ownership includes safety, compliance, operations, and retirement authority.

Overlay evidence should cite a durable URL and owner, then name the next review
date. An overlay must never turn “not applicable” into a shortcut; the rationale
still has to explain why the universal pressure genuinely does not apply.
